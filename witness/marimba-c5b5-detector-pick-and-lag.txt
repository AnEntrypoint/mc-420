5. WHERE THE 83 c ACTUALLY COMES FROM
   tools/loopback-witness/loopback-witness.js holds one keybed note for
   5.25 s and reports ONE number: estimateF0(settleSegment(captured,
   noteOnMs)), a single whole-segment estimate over ~5.5 s (line 484, 531).
   That window covers note 1 and note 2 of the run. The lock holds note 1's
   interval (72 - 72.16 = -0.16 semitones) through note 2's re-acquisition:
   energyReady needs 1680 samples (35 ms) above floor and the coarse pick
   then needs ~30 ms to settle, so note 2's first ~68 ms passes through at
   558.94 * 2^(-0.16/12) = 553.85 Hz. That first 68 ms is the mallet attack
   and carries more energy (env 0.12) than the whole 0.7 s that follows
   (env 0.0525 -> 0.0027), so it dominates the single estimate:
     measured 548.43 / 548.21 / 545.96 Hz, predicted 553.85 Hz = -17 c
   The 83 c is therefore a run-average artifact of a multi-note file read
   with a single-segment estimator, not a detector bias.

6. WHY NO DETECTOR CHANGE CLOSES IT
   - coarse grid: the pick is already correct (step 2); the user froze it.
   - demote margin: the demote never fires on this file (step 2); frozen.
   - refineSpreadK: at L=81 K=8 and the vertex lands 0.04 samples off the
     true period; changing K moves a -1.1 c residual, and dfd4871a80 must
     not regress the C6/A2/A3 set.
   - onsetHoldMs 35.0: this is the only detector knob in the path, and it
     buys at most ~25 ms of the ~68 ms unshifted attack. It exists to stop
     a fresh onset reading a spurious high-octave or floor candidate
     (documented in the tracker's own description) and lowering it trades
     a documented octave-lock failure for ~25 ms.
   Re-acquisition cannot reach a few ms: corrAtLagTau's estimator is a
   10 ms one-pole and 20 ms at >=575 Hz, which is the floor on any onset
   lock. No change here moves 548.43 to the 538.9 Hz the acceptance needs
   (50 c over 523.25) without one of those regressions.

# Voice-path profile, reported by the DawDreamer session (not reproduced here)

Provenance: measured by a peer session on an aarch64 Pi-class host, one core (taskset -c 3), 48 kHz, steady_clock, 6 GB address-space cap, Faust 2.81.10, pinned flags. A second compile ran on another core during the runs, so absolute numbers carry noise. Files are in that session's scratchpad (not committed); hashes below are as reported.

ITEM 3, multitranspose.dsp process(), 512-sample blocks (10.667 ms budget), 220 Hz sine, extFreqDet=220, p50 block time:
- 0 held voices: 339-346 us (3.2%); FFI stubbed 172 us (1.6%)
- 1 held voice: 375-378 us (3.5%); stubbed 170 us (1.6%)
- 6 held voices: 537-588 us (5.0-5.5%); stubbed 165 us (1.5%)
- 55 Hz input: 0 voices 388 us (3.6%), 6 voices 554 us (5.2%)
- Idle voices cost about 60% of six voices. voiceActive only mutes the output (output peak 0.000 with 0 voices). SnacPeriodTracker tick() and confidence() run every sample regardless of gates; six active voices add about 235 us (engines plus formant).

ITEM 5, audio_thread.cpp per-block bookkeeping (lines 1128-1165 fold/latency, 1245-1315 guard, 61 telemetry stores), same loop bodies copied verbatim, stubbed params and zones, 2,000,000 iterations:
- fold: 224-232 ns/block at N=64 up to 1533-1626 at N=512 (about 3 ns per sample, 0.015-0.017% of a core)
- guard: 38-47 ns/block, fixed (0.0004-0.003%)
- 61 telemetry stores: 50-57 ns/block, fixed
- all three together about 0.02% of a core: negligible

CONCLUSION: the only cost worth a look is the always-on tracker and confidence call when no voice is held (about 1.6 points of a core at 220 Hz). Any change there must leave the lock output unchanged and add no latency; the SNAC sweep bound (MAX_PERIOD 900, LAGS_PER_STEP 53) must not be cut.

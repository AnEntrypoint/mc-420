LIKELY CAUSE (not measured)
- tauFor: each c(f) call site passes a literal frequency, so the nested ba.if is constant-folded. Unlikely to be the cost.
- corrAtLagVar: VA uses a constant pole. The VP variants make the pole a signal, ba.tau2pole(max(0.01, K*lag/SR)), where lag is runtime. corrAtLagVar is instantiated at about 15 call sites (refineFreq, corrPeakAt, corrRawAt). Each instance now carries an exp per sample and a variable-coefficient feedback recursion (+ ~ *(pole)) whose coefficient depends on coarseFreq. Expansion multiplies per instance. This is the probable cause.

PROPOSED CHEAPER FORM (all poles compile-time constants; no per-sample exp; no signal-valued recursion coefficient)
- corrAtLagVar selects one of three precomputed poles by lag band:
    pole = ba.if(lagClamped <= ma.SR/100.0, P_A, ba.if(lagClamped <= ma.SR/50.0, P_B, P_C));
    P_A = ba.tau2pole(0.01)          (band f >= 100 Hz: identical to main)
    P_B = ba.tau2pole(K/70.0)        (band 50 <= f < 100 Hz)
    P_C = ba.tau2pole(K/35.0)        (band f < 50 Hz)
- Constants (tau in seconds):
    K=1.0: P_B 0.014286, P_C 0.028571
    K=1.5: P_B 0.021429, P_C 0.042857
    K=2.0: P_B 0.028571, P_C 0.057143
- Caveat: inside each band P_B and P_C are exact only at their representative frequency. The whole low grid (50.5 to 62.64 Hz) falls in band B, so the 55.69 Hz sine is smoothed with tau K/70, not K/55.69. This is a different variant from the spec and would be named VPB, not VP10/15/20.
- Exact-band alternative, if the approximation is too coarse: one constant per low-grid candidate (50.5, 52.73, 55.05, 57.47, 60, 62.64) with tau K/f, chosen by lag window, plus 0.01 for f >= 100 Hz. Still constant, no exp, but a 7-way ba.if chain per Var instance. Check codegen cost before any compile of it.
- corrAtLagTau keeps the VA tauFor (0.01 for 100 to 700 Hz, 0.02 at or above 700 Hz, unchanged). Its literal call sites already fold any K/f term, so this part of the cheap form matches the VP files.

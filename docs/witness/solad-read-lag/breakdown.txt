WHY DUBFX_BS IS NOT THE KNOB
  The SNAC sweep steps on BLOCK=64 (snacPeriodTracker.h:14, :68-69) and
  soladSnacOctaver.h:116 calls stepSchedule(n) once per processBlock, so a
  32-sample chunk double-steps the sweep. AGENTS.md: step MUST stay
  block-aligned (m_sinceBlock==0).

Upward-shift fold-back refuted

  • Upward-shift clicks are NOT fold-back aliasing. tools/upshift-fold-map/fold_map.py measured it: over 16 sources x 5 shifts, Pearson r(glitches, out-of-band energy above fs/(2s)) is -0.04 pooled, and every per-column r is negative. SNAC cap 900 below 60 Hz

commit 8ed5946 body, verbatim

pitch: SNAC tracker sees below 60 Hz, gated

MAX_PERIOD 800 caps f0 at 60 Hz at 48 kHz. piano_low_A1 is A1 at 55.69 Hz, true period 861.9, outside the range, so the tracker never sees it: dsp_cli –trk reports mean 1116.1 cents, worst 1667.3, and the trace wanders 450-780 with peakTau often 16.

Ceiling raised to 900 (f0 53.3 Hz). A period of 862 leaves 162 of the 1024-sample window, 1.19 periods; that is enough, the file locks at 59.7 cents mean, 824.6 worst.

LAGS_PER_STEP goes 48 -> 53 in the same commit. The sweep advances one step per 64-sample block and must still finish in 17 of them: 1+1748 = 817 > 800 and 1+1753 = 902 > 900, so detection lands on the same sample as before. Without it the sweep takes 19 blocks, every detection window shifts 128 samples, and 11 of 19 files move for timing reasons alone, which hides what the range change does. With it, 14 of 19 are identical.

The new lags are gated, mirroring SHORT_LAG_STRICT_PERIOD: LONG_LAG_STRICT_PERIOD = 800 is the characterized ceiling, and everything that sets a threshold from the curve (rPeak, the global CMNDF minimum, the raw-correlation peak) is computed over that range only. A lag at or above 800 is reachable only when that range shows no periodicity at all (refMin > kDiffAccept) and only through a CMNDF minimum below kDiffStrict, never through a raw correlation peak. cello_low_sulC_A2 (112.01 Hz, 2x period 857, the one file whose octave-down lands inside the new range) is byte-identical: no octave-down adoption.

Bare 900 without the gates is a large regression: oboe_mid_C5B5 376.5 -> 1760.8, vibraphone_mid_C5B5 165.3 -> 1441.0, soprano_sax_high_C6 2.8 -> 1233.7, violin_high_sulE_C7E7 126.3 -> 1799.7.

piano_low_A1 renders at normrms=0.21, cents vs measured input f0: ncc 0.3832 -> 0.5655 (+12), 0.2831 -> 0.5109 (+19), 0.2766 -> 0.5136 (+24) splices 294 -> 192, 575 -> 374, 859 -> 564 cents -0.9 / -2.7 / -1.7, was +1.1 / -1.7 / -1.2

Honest counter-number: glitch count on this one file moves the other way, 24 -> 27 at +12 and 27 -> 28 at +19, 31 -> 29 at +24. Every hit on it is a 0.25-0.31 first difference after normrms=0.21 scales a peak-0.0757 source about 30x, clustered at t=0.48-0.70 s in the base render too, so the count sits on the threshold and a large coherence win moves it either way. violin_high_sulE_C7E7 reads 7 -> 10 and 10 -> 14 glitches the same way, deltas 0.25-0.32.

9 EXACT cells unchanged: vocal_female_vibrato 0/21/52, piano_mid_C4 1/3/2, marimba_mid_C5B5 4/5/5.

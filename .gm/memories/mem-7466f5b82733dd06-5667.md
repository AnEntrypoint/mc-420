LpcFormantShifter (vowelFormant.h) design/verification history, drained from AGENTS.md during compaction.

Overall accuracy: measured over 72 cases with formant actually applied, worst error went from 1904 cents to 14.4 cents, cases beyond +-25c from 9 to 0. Over a wider 280-case sweep (f0 82-880Hz x 7 shifts x 5 formant depths, output 41Hz-1760Hz): zero new failures, zero cases worse than the previous (GrainFormant) build, 15 cases the previous build got wrong that are now right, total beyond +-25c 35 to 20 (every one of the 20 remaining also wrong in the previous build, nothing regressed). Neutral formant bit-identical over 30 cases. 175 numerical stress cases (silence, +-DC, denormal, full-scale square, clipped noise, formant +-3.0) give zero non-finite samples and nothing outside the structural ceiling.

Two design corrections that cost real investigation: LPC order 10-12 is the 8kHz speech figure and is useless at 48kHz (order 12 measured as a complete no-op, orders 20/32 miss F2 at 1090Hz entirely; order 44 recovers [698,1086,2441] against a true 730/1090/2440). Warping the autocorrelation in the LAG domain aliases past Nyquist (a factor-of-2 warp produced spurious envelope peaks BELOW the source, 326Hz from a 946Hz source) -- fixed by warping the model power spectrum on an explicit bin grid with band-limited HF fill instead. Real cost is ~24 MACs/sample/voice for the filtering, not the 'well under 10' originally estimated.

High-fundamental defect: an LPC pair is a linear filter and cannot move a partial. When the fundamental is high, warping the envelope moves the modelled resonances off the only two or three harmonics that exist and annihilates H1 (measured at f0=880, +7 semitones, formant +1.0: H1 drops 36dB, H2 becomes dominant, an octave percept). Fires in both warp directions, tracks OUTPUT frequency not shift amount. Bounding the LPC order by harmonic count was tried and refuted: at order 8-9 there's no harmonic-fitting left to do and the defect is undiminished (worst error 2407c, still 6-9 bad cases). Current mitigation is three bounds: a warp taper against output pitch (full below 450Hz, zero by 900Hz), a fundamental-level protection clamping the warped spectrum to within 1.5dB of the source below 2x the fundamental, and an order bound (helpful, not sufficient alone). Measured on a formant-bearing vowel stimulus (F1/F2/F3=730/1090/2440, f0=150Hz): the old GrainFormant-era shaper commanded down 1.5 octaves moved F1 the WRONG WAY (764Hz->855Hz) and was non-monotonic upward; the LPC stage moves F1 down correctly and is monotonic upward. Widening the fundamental-protection span from 1.25x to 2.0x was needed to clear cases at 494Hz/698Hz and cost some downward movement magnitude at 330/440Hz -- correctness chosen over movement magnitude.

Hardware CPU cost history (superseded by this session's own fresher measurement, see the beginBlock cadence fix): an earlier x86-only figure (49.6/51.0us per block before LPC, 87.6/97.7us after, 6 voices) was later validated on real Pi 4 hardware via byte-level MIDI injection sweeping fx/formant across MultiKey transpose with 6 held keys, showing 80-83% peak core_busy with 2-3 gaps and 0 xruns -- about 3 percentage points of cost at either formant extreme versus neutral, not the 1.8x the x86 number predicted, because the per-voice analysis-phase staggering does its job on target. That 80-83% baseline was itself measured BEFORE the DUBFX_POLY_BS 64->16 perf change (1b70abb) and its associated beginBlock cadence regression/fix -- see the vowelFormant.h beginBlock section for the current, lower measured cost.

Known disclosed corner: at f0=880 with -12 semitones the output reads +1200c in both the old and LPC builds including at formant=0 where they're bit-identical -- a pre-existing splice-path failure at that corner, unrelated to formants. The LPC build drives H1 deeper there (-37.1dB vs -18.6dB) without changing the verdict. Reasoning (not measured): with SNAC unlocked the output period holds its last valid value so the taper uses a stale pitch, and unpitched material has no harmonic comb for the defect to act on.

Historical, now superseded -- VowelFormantShaper (3 parallel RBJ peaking biquads tuned to F1/F2/F3 of a 5-point vowel table: U/O/A/E/I from {300,870,2240}/{570,840,2410}/{730,1090,2440}/{530,1840,2480}/{270,2290,3010} Hz) no longer exists in vowelFormant.h -- it was the 'three peaking-EQ boosts' LpcFormantShifter replaced. Its design intent (formant depth double-duties as both grain-mix depth and vowel-filter blend depth, sign/position maps onto the U-O-A-I-E continuum) is superseded now that GrainFormant is entirely inert on the poly path and LpcFormantShifter's own warp mechanism does the formant coloring.

SibilanceDetector's HF-ratio bug and fix (this part IS still current -- SibilanceDetector remains a real struct in vowelFormant.h): the ungated detector fired full-scale (peak 1.0) on both a guitar pick attack and a sustained guitar note, because a pick attack is exactly the broadband HF burst it looks for. Its one-pole highpass was also wrong rather than merely leaky (hp=0.30*(hpState+x-xPrev) has a gain of only 0.46 at Nyquist, attenuating the very band it measures; real sibilance scored a mean of just 0.035). Fixed to a unity-at-Nyquist form (hp=p*hpState+((1+p)/2)*(x-xPrev), p=0.5925, corner ~4kHz -- this IS the shipped kHpPole constant). Measured after: guitar pick attack and sustained guitar note both 0.000, genuine unpitched sibilance 0.988 mean. A pitched instrument holds its SNAC lock through a pick transient (m_lockMiss needs 3 consecutive misses), which is what makes the gate discriminate.

Reported as: the punch varispeed buttons do not work, they change right back. Fixed 2026-09-06. The Link phase trim was the thing changing them back. A manual half/double-speed punch deliberately runs playback away from the Link grid. But masterPhaseSamples advanced at linkSpeedRatio ALONE, without g_manualSpeedMul, so pressing half-speed halved the read rate while the target timeline kept full rate. Phase error grew immediately, the trim saturated at its clamp trying to close it, and the loop was dragged back to the grid within a fraction of a second. dsp/loop.dsp already anticipates this: manualPunchActive = abs(effSpeed-1.0) > 0.3 zeroes its own resyncCoeff so the DSP internal resync never fights a punch, and AGENTS.md records that the abrupt punch character is intentional and must not be smoothed. Any NEW correction added in C++ must mirror that rule rather than inventing a different one. Fix has two halves: suspend the trim while abs(g_manualSpeedMul-1) > 0.3, and advance masterPhaseSamples at linkSpeedRatio * g_manualSpeedMul so the target follows the punch and no error accumulates from the punch itself. Design note worth keeping: the trim is added to effSpeed but deliberately NOT to the masterPhaseSamples advance. masterPhaseSamples is the TARGET timeline (what Link says); the trim is the correction applied to the READER to walk it toward that target. Adding the trim to both would make the target run away from the reader and never converge. Verified live by driving note70 (cmd/halfspeed) and note71 (cmd/doublespeed) over tcp/9401 and reading eff_speed from /run/aloop/status.json: 0.5000 held stable across 1.5s, 2.0000 likewise, both returning to exactly 1.0000 on release.

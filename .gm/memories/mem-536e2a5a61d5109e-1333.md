test/hardware/verify-quantization.js -- byte-level verification of the loop-length quantization spec. POSSIBLE DISCREPANCY worth checking: this script's header states the extend-vs-trim decision between bracketing power-of-2 candidates uses a '68% extend-vs-trim threshold', while AGENTS.md's 'Successive-recording quantization' section describes it as the LOG-SPACE GEOMETRIC MIDPOINT (sqrt(lowerCand*upperCand)) -- these may be the same rule described two different ways (a geometric midpoint between candidates a factor of 2 apart does NOT land at the 50% linear point), or one of the two descriptions may be stale; worth reconciling against the real dsp/loop.dsp or apc_grid.cpp source before trusting either verbatim. Reads back the actual latched wrapLen via udp/4445's telemetry query (src/control/telemetry.cpp) -- any non-empty UDP payload triggers a status JSON reply, not a specific query string. The control thread's ~5Hz poll loop (main.cpp usleep(200ms)) means a fresh telemetry snapshot reflecting a just-applied finish can take up to ~2 full poll periods (~400ms) to publish -- wait comfortably past that before reading. The script's own MIDI-note-for-looper-index helper only covers loopers 0-3 (row 0 of the grid, note=col 2..5); extending to looper 4+ needs the real row/col math from apc_grid.h's gridLooperIndex.

Resonode (effects/home/faust/resonode_synth.dsp) investigation history, drained from AGENTS.md during compaction -- current-state facts stayed in AGENTS.md, this is the 'why'/measurement history behind them.

EXCITER REDESIGN: original design pre-filtered the shared mic tap through a per-voice fi.resonbp(freqHz,15.0,1.0) Q=15 bandpass locked to the played note's fundamental BEFORE the 6-mode split. Measured via DawDreamer JIT (test/resonode-sweetspot/): this made modes 2-6 receive 30-190x less excitation energy than mode 1 regardless of position/stretch/damping -- the '6-mode' bank was functionally a single resonant filter (spectral centroid pinned to fundamental for every patch even 'bright/inharmonic' ones, narrowband audio-feedback-like ringing instead of a struck object). Fixed: sharedExciteIn is now a SHARED (not per-voice) highpass(60Hz)+lowpass(tone) only -- matches Gabriel Soule's Resonarium thesis and Reason Studios' Objekt manual (exciter modules deliberately broadband, resonator bank does mode selection). modeGain6 raised 0.16->0.55 to close a remaining ~30x mode6 gap (a b0/r interaction at long T60 the numerator normalization doesn't fully equalize) -- empirically tuned, re-verify with verify_musical_controls.py's modes_2_to_6_are_not_starved before changing.

EXCITER ENVELOPE: was en.asr(0.02,1.0,0.3,xgate) -- 20ms attack then FULL sustain 1.0 for the whole held note, i.e. a continuously-open high-Q filter re-processing live mic input for the whole note, structurally an audio feedback loop shape. Fixed to en.adsr(0.004,0.11,0.09,0.25,xgate): 4ms attack, decay to 9%-of-peak sustain floor over 110ms, 250ms release. Measured via DawDreamer against continuous moderate broadband noise (room-tone/mic-bleed stand-in): settled long-hold RMS vs onset peak dropped from ~92% (old) to ~40% (new), with a genuine dip to ~14% in the 100-300ms post-strike window -- a real measured hit-then-settle contour that didn't exist before.

COUPLING REDESIGN (two generations): Gen 1 -- nearest-neighbor coupling (mode k with k-1/k+1, Faust letrec over all 6 mode outputs; a scattered x' reference across independent with{} blocks hits an 'endless evaluation cycle' Faust compile failure, must be one letrec block) modeled on Resonarium 'interlinked'/Objekt 'Coupling: On/X-Over'. Nearest-neighbor (O(modes), 5 cross-terms/voice) chosen over fully-connected (O(modes^2), 30 cross-terms) per both reference docs' cost-vs-richness guidance. coupleScale=8.0 empirically chosen (smallest value giving ~20% audible peak-sample/centroid effect at couple=1.0 without dominating patch identity). A real divergence bug was found: the claim 'cannot diverge by construction' was WRONG -- each mode filter alone (r<1) is BIBO-stable but a real linear feedback loop through 2+ coupled modes can have combined loop gain above unity at some corners even with every pole damped. verify_musical_controls.py's old checks only used ~15ms burst excitation into silence, never seeing divergence that only emerges after continuous excitation (a real mic input never stops exciting the loop, unlike a burst). A DawDreamer sweep with SUSTAINED noise excitation over a multi-second held gate found genuine NaN/Inf runaway at position=0.08,decay=0.15,damping=0.97,stretch=-0.4,couple=1.0,note=36 (and nearby corners), diverging ~1.5s into a held note (slow blow-up, not instant). Fixed (Gen 1) by wrapping every mode-to-mode coupling READ in coupleFeedback(x)=tanh(x*0.25)*4.0 -- near-identity at normal levels, strictly bounded for large values, provably closes the divergence gap. Re-verified: 2/288 diverging corners -> 0, all existing checks unchanged.

Gen 2 (current shipped): tanh guard replaced with skew-symmetric energy exchange on peak-normalized states -- each mode exports rawOut*aliasGuard/modePeakGain(r) (peak-normalized so loop gain is decay-independent), network computes u_{k+1}-u_{k-1} (skew/lossless exchange) scaled by couple*0.45, hard min/max clamp at +-8 as last-resort guard only. modePeakGain(r)=sqrt(K(1+r)/(1-r)) checked against exact filter response over whole (T60,f) range (max error 0.14%), giving a real small-gain bound 2c<=0.901<1. Both symmetric and skew exchange were built and measured over 72 sustained corners against a guard-free twin: symmetric gives 0.22% couple effect, SKEW gives 12.84% (~58x more audible) with zero clamping/saturation even at 4.4x the symmetric bound -- skew chosen. coupleSmallGainMax=0.45 kept as a proven bound, not just empirical. Hard clamp chosen over tanh deliberately: tanh is never exactly identity so 'never engages' is unprovable; hard clamp is exact identity below threshold. Probed over 150 corners: sum over all modes of |normalized coupling read| peaks at 0.583 against +-8.0 clamp (13.7x headroom). A separation-aware/frequency-aware coupling bound via the Lorentzian formula max|H_k*H_k'|<=G_kG_k'/(1+(df/(B_k+B_k'))^2) was tested over 4000 random (T60,f) pairs and VIOLATED by up to 7619x -- the (1-z^-2) numerator's far-field tails are not Lorentzian, do not re-propose.

CPU/MODE-COUNT OPTIMIZATION LADDER: at 24 modes with Chebyshev sine in place, real hardware test (4 voices held) ran 94% core_busy, readi gaps to 8.06ms (vs 1.333ms budget), 70 xruns/15s; 8-key stress hit 101-110%. Cost is FLAT across 1-4 voices held (Faust has no runtime branching -- all voice banks compute every sample regardless) -- even 0 keys held with Resonode engaged cost 122 diag-gap events + 42 xruns/10s vs zero disengaged. Three sound-preserving optimizations tried, verified via faust-codegen-probe workflow (real generated C++, not source-level argument): (1) SHIPPED -- position sine as Chebyshev polynomial: sin(pi*pos*(i+1))'s multiplier is always-integer mode index, so sin(k*t)=sin(t)*U_{k-1}(cos t) applies (U_i, Chebyshev 2nd kind), sin call count 64->4, max abs error 1.03e-14 (double)/4.6e-6 (shipped float, ~-107dBFS, inaudible) -- re-check this float error if modeCount is ever raised since the recurrence's error grows with mode index. (2) REVERTED -- two per-mode sqrt calls do cancel algebraically (b0/modePeakGain=|1-r|, max rel error 7.4e-13) BUT the refactor moving b0 from a numerator coefficient inside fi.tf2 to a post-multiply outside is NOT sound-preserving when b0 varies at audio rate (it does, via morphGlide): measured 8.4% peak divergence during a pole glide (vs 3.6e-12% at rest), persisting well past the 15ms glide through the resonator's multi-second tail -- correctness chosen over the CPU win. (3) REVERTED -- de-gliding the five shape weights hoists log 12->1 calls but those weights feed both freqMode and poleRadius, so an ungated slider step would move a1/a2 in one sample on a resonator holding up to 8.5s of stored energy at r~0.96-0.99998 -- a real audible step/zipper on a ringing filter, rejected same as the project's existing anti-zipper policy for morph knobs. Measured ladder (8-key stress, peak core_busy/gaps): 12 modes ~73%/2-3gaps, 16 modes 70-73%/5-7, 20 modes 82-85%/8-13, 24 modes WILL NOT BUILD (see below). Shipped build (Chebyshev sine only, sqrt/shape-weight changes reverted): 0 keys=0 gaps, 8-key stress 34-73%peak/0-2gaps, worst case (Resonode+guitar+lofi+8keys) 80%peak/3gaps, zero xruns throughout, worst single gap 6.0ms vs 2.3ms typical, zero crash-watchdog trips -- the Chebyshev sine + 24->16 mode reduction alone carry the budget; the two reverted optimizations were never load-bearing. modeCount=24 hits a Faust COMPILE-TIME ceiling independent of CPU: with Chebyshev sine in place, real faust compiler killed by SIGALRM ~2min into resonode_synth.dsp's -i -a lv2.cpp codegen (exit 142 'Alarm clock') -- this project's documented compile-time cliff, reproduced not assumed. modeCount ships at 16 (roughly 10pp more headroom than 20 for 4 fewer partials). Three further optimizations tried and REFUTED by measurement, do not re-propose: rewriting exp(modeLogRatio*e) as pow(modeRatio,e) is numerically exact but WORSE (576->679 per-sample transcendentals, since pow expands to log+exp internally); applying the Chebyshev recurrence to the resonator's own cos(2*pi*f/SR) is exact only for INTEGER mode ratios (5.6e-3 error against the bell table, breaks 4 of 5 timbres, only 'string' is integer); un-gliding the six morph knobs bought 576->573 (nothing) and would cost zipper-free sweeps. The remaining ~120 per-sample exp calls are not hoistable: they're exp(fSlow_k*fZec[i]) where the generated ratioExponent=fRec22+0.02*fRec73+1.0 has fRec22 (glided stretch, recursive) and fRec73 (stretchJitter sample-and-hold, also recursive) both audio-rate regardless of the jitter term. Moving jitter to the fundamental instead was considered and rejected on sound grounds: in the exponent it leaves the fundamental dead-in-tune while smearing upper partials up to 107 cents (a timbral variation, kept); on the fundamental directly it's a rigid 34-cent retune reading as pitch instability (rejected). modes_2_to_6_are_not_starved fails as literally written after the position-drift change (28.0x vs 15x bound) -- proven to be a measurement artifact by isolation: with positionDriftAmt=0 the worst ratio drops to 8.7x (inside bound), stretchJitter contributes nothing. The shipped position-drift envelope sweeps positionLive ~0.24->0.08 across the check's own 10-90ms window, so high-index modes cross a sin(pi*position*k) null and partially cancel in the windowed FFT; the old baseline only passed at 9.7x because of the mode-6 0.55 bump (2.5x factor, 9.7*2.5=24 matching the failure). Restoring a mode-6-shaped bump would turn the check green without fixing anything real -- not done.

DISTINCTNESS MEASUREMENT: the 5 named ratio tables (string/bell/plate/membrane/bar) produce genuinely different timbres, not relabeled copies -- measured at note 52/decay 4.0/damping 0.97, centroid/inharmonic-fraction: string 1490Hz/0.21, bell 1393Hz/0.51, plate 1794Hz/0.53, membrane 703Hz/0.76, bar 2499Hz/0.59, minimum pairwise max-relative-feature difference 0.224. Named sweetspot patches (kResonodePatches) were found via a real DawDreamer grid search (625 combos of position/decay/damping/stretch x5 levels) scored against measured features (decay time, transient ratio, spectral centroid, LF-energy ratio) vs a hand-authored target direction per voice; collision values are hand-set judgment calls, not swept. The table's raw values didn't need retuning after the exciter/coupling fixes -- the OLD engine's mode-2-6 starvation was masking differentiation the values already encoded (early-transient centroid across the 4 patches went from flat ~220Hz/1.0x-fundamental pre-fix to a real 340-1300Hz/1.7-5.9x spread post-fix with zero table changes).
